Dropdown submenus show a right chevron again

The app's icon settings gave every menu an inline menu's down chevron, which turned up when a submenu opened, and it replaced the dropdown blocks' own arrow. `DropdownMenu`, `DropdownButton` and the header's profile and locale menus now show a right chevron on items with a submenu.
