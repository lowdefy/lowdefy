fix(deps): Update lucide and lucide-react to 1.72.0

Lucide 1.72.0 adds new icons, including cheese, moped, mango, laser, earbuds, globe-ban, database-cog, chart-quadrant and square-dashed-search. Icons Lucide renamed in these releases (such as archive-restore, now archive-arrow-up, and the *-alert and *-warning icons, now *-exclamation-point) keep working under their old names. The built-in semantic icon names `warning`, `icon-missing` and `bold` now point at Lucide's new names for the same icons (TriangleExclamationPoint, CircleExclamationPoint and LetterB), so they draw exactly as before.
